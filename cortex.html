Puzzle Mode

flip · self-rate · let the tricky ones resurface
How this works
Learn with the Leitner system
The idea: each card sits in one of five boxes. Tap a card to flip it, then be honest — "Still learning" sends it back to Box 1 for a quick retest, while "Got it" promotes it to the next box, where it's spaced further apart before it shows up again. This is the same active-recall + spaced-repetition technique behind apps like Anki, and it works because forcing your brain to retrieve an answer (not just re-read it) is what actually builds long-term memory.
